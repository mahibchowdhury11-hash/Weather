South America

Temperature

The range for the average temperature in South America is from 9.73 degrees celcius all the way 27.60 degrees celcius. The range isn't too big and we can see that South America is a pretty hot continent.

Rainfall

The average rainfall in South America ranges from 600 milimetres in Argentina all the way to 2561 milimetres from Colombia. We can say that there is alot of rain on South America.
