Africa

Temperature

Africa's average temperature per year from different countries ranges from 18.91 degrees celcius all the way to 29.94 degrees celcius. But that is excluding Lesotho which has an average temperature of 13.21 which makes the country an outliar. But in conclusion we can say that Africa is a pretty warm country.

Rainfall

The average rainfall per year in Africa ranges from only 15.58 milimeters all the way to 2179 milimeters. So we can tell that Africa has a really big range and difference between its countries. Equatorial Guinea has the highest rainfall and Egpyt has the lowest amount of rainfall.
