North America

Temperature

North America's average temperature range is from -2.98 degrees celcius from Canada all the way to 29.63 degrees celcius from Aruba but if we aren't counting Central America thaat the highest temperature is 22.68 degrees celcius from Mexico.

Rainfall

The average rainfall per year in North America is ranging from 530 milimetres all the way to 3300 milimetres. The range isn't too big compared to the other continents.
